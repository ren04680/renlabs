What it will not do

  • Quote a placement average. The university publishes none with a cohort year, so Rehan says so rather than repeating a headline number.
  • Say admissions are open. The 2026–27 cycle closed in August 2026. It gives the one live deadline instead.
  • Guess a fee. Where a figure isn't published, it says so and gives the admissions number.
  • Hide a contradiction. Where two official pages disagree, it names the conflict instead of picking one.
